Accounting automation ↗

I wanted to follow a journal entry from the classroom exercise to the general ledger. I built a practice environment where I can review and approve entries for Hanbit Trading, a fictional wholesaler.

How a sale reaches the ledger

An AI app uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to retrieve a transaction and its supporting documents. The accounting engine drafts the entry and calculates cost of goods sold from the quantity shipped and its unit cost.

A reviewer checks the debits, credits, and supporting documents before approving the entry. The engine posts the entry only if it still matches the approved version.

Property search for my mother’s real estate office ↗

I built a search page for the properties my mother’s office lists in Hyangnam. She tried it, told me what to change, and I revised the interface.

What happens when someone searches for a jeonse apartment?

I connected the office domain to Cloudflare Workers. Selecting “jeonse” and “apartment” puts both filters in the URL. Astro reads them and retrieves the matching public listings from Supabase.

Reopening the URL shows the listings currently on the market. For jeonse and monthly rentals, the price filter uses the deposit amount.
