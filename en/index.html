Hanbit Trading accounting lab ↗

I built a practice ledger for a fictional wholesaler. Learners follow a sale from its invoice and delivery record through approval and posting, so they can see how the evidence supports each journal entry.

How a sale reaches the ledger

An AI client retrieves the sale and its supporting documents through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). The accounting engine uses the quantity shipped and the unit cost to calculate cost of goods sold. It checks the tax invoice against the delivery record and drafts entries for the sale and the decrease in inventory.

The engine checks the debit and credit totals and the supporting documents. The learner can then approve or reject the entry. At posting, the engine verifies that the entry matches the approved version.

Real estate

Shinyoung Gwell’s Hyangnam property search ↗

I built a website for my mother’s brokerage so clients can search its public listings by transaction type and property type. During a consultation, staff can share a link and review the same results with the client.

How listing searches work

I connected the office’s domain to Cloudflare Workers. The Astro site reads the search filters from the URL and queries Supabase for matching public listings.

A client can share or reopen a search and see the listings that match at that time. The price filter uses the asking price for sales and the deposit for jeonse and monthly rentals.
