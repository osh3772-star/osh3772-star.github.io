Accounting project · Simulated workflow

Sales rebate review and cash planning

I built a rebate review app using 240 settlement records from 50 fictional dealers. The approval rule applies to each related group’s monthly total. I implemented that rule and added a way to plan for cash shortfalls between approval and payment.

Why three dealers needed a different approver

Sales support could approve up to KRW 2 million per month under the rules of the exercise. I compared dealers’ registered representatives, business addresses and settlement accounts. I linked dealers that matched on at least two of the three and grouped the connected records.

The cash planning feature I added

After a rebate is approved, the finance team checks the funds available on the payment date. If cash falls short, AI suggests funding options. When a user selects a collections plan, the app assigns sales support to confirm the collection date and finance to reconcile the deposit.
